03 · Scientific foundations

Models, assumptions and verification

Make the mathematics and limits visible, without turning the main dashboard into an instruction manual.

Geometric Brownian Motion

Constant daily log-return parameters μ = 0.000615 and σ = 0.028460 estimated from 2022–2024. Each day uses a seeded independent normal shock; prices evolve multiplicatively.

ln(Pₜ₊₁/Pₜ) = μ + σZₜ

Two-state Markov model

Calm/volatile log-return means and standard deviations differ by state. State transitions use 499/540 for Calm→Calm and 500/541 for Volatile→Volatile. Overlapping 14-day volatility windows mechanically inflate apparent persistence.

rₜ ∼ Normal(μ_state, σ_state²)

EWMA volatility benchmark

Variance evolves from simulated log-return shocks, not unseen future prices. The decay λ=0.90 was selected using 2024. Every EWMA slider starting variance is from the executed notebook using 2022–2024 information only; simulated shocks update variance after the forecast origin.

vₜ₊₁ = λvₜ + (1−λ)(rₜ−μ)²

How to use CryptoRisk Lab

CryptoRisk Lab examines how different assumptions about Bitcoin volatility change the distribution of possible losses. It does not predict tomorrow's live market.

  1. Configure. Choose an investment, 30-day horizon, Monte Carlo path count, loss threshold and volatility scenario. Advanced settings include the seed and EWMA decay.
  2. Run. The simulator generates independent seeded GBM, Markov and EWMA scenarios. Computation progress appears beside the controls.
  3. Watch. Forecast paths and percentile fans extend day by day from the real 31 Dec 2024 closing price. Use Pause, Play, Replay and the day slider to inspect the same generated paths.
  4. Explore the chart. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag horizontally to pan, drag the right price axis for vertical zoom, and hover to see the exact price level alongside candle values. Double-click to fit the forecast or double-click the price scale to reset vertical zoom. Keyboard: Left/Right pan, Up/Down zoom, Home fits forecast, End fits 2025. Use Reveal actual 2025 to inspect the entire observed year and Fit forecast to zoom back to the original simulation; no forecast is extended beyond the selected horizon.
  5. Interpret risk. Terminal-loss probability is measured at day N. Daily-close touch probability asks whether the loss boundary was crossed at any simulated close. VaR95 and ES95 describe the estimated loss tail.
  6. Validate. Historical Brier scores are fixed notebook results. They are not recalculated from your scenario sliders and do not establish which model will perform best in future markets.

Important limitations

These Monte Carlo paths use historical parameters, normal log-return shocks and no fees, leverage or market frictions. The Markov classification's persistence is partly mechanical. EWMA decay sensitivities retain full-history origin states, but do not establish real-world superiority. Past forecasts and simulations are not financial advice.
