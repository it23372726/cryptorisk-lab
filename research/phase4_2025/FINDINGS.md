## Generalization and limitations

The separated 2026 holdout evaluated the same 2022–2024 frozen models and found **no statistically established improvement** from Adaptive GBM or GARCH over constant GBM on the ≥10% metric.
The two study years are different market samples with different loss frequencies, so raw Brier levels across years cannot be interpreted as model-performance changes without context.
The 335 rolling 30-day windows are strongly overlapping; there were no ≥35% events.
Gaussian shocks can misrepresent fat-tailed BTC outcomes.
